# RB06: Duplicate debit

## RB06.1 Signals
- `duplicate_of_ref` is set, meaning this debit repeats an earlier transfer

## RB06.2 Meaning
The customer may have been charged twice for one intended transfer.

## RB06.3 Action
Always escalate to Reconciliation. A duplicate needs a manual review and a manual reversal decision. Agents can't confirm a refund.

## RB06.4 Customer-safe wording
"We can see what looks like a repeated debit, and we've passed it to our reconciliation team for review. We'll update you once they've checked it."
